## What Was Discussed and Decided

* The project will involve sensitive public data related to corporate actions.
* BNY’s custody group receives large-scale public data and translates it into appropriate corporate actions.
* When a corporate action occurs, the goal is to ensure that the correct decision or follow-up action is taken.
* The team should conduct research on the problem statement, existing solutions, and possible approaches before focusing solely on implementation.
* The project should be approached like a startup product or SaaS solution. This includes considering product design, return on investment, scalability, industry impact, and the potential effect on BNY.
* The team should focus on developing a technical solution while exploring multiple possible approaches.
* Research and experimentation are considered as important as writing code. The project should include new ideas and clearly documented experiments.
* The team may create and use synthetic data if necessary.
* The team can explore new tools and does not need to focus immediately on integrating the solution with BNY’s existing technology stack.
* Weekly meetings with BNY will be held for approximately 45 minutes. The team will provide relevant updates on research and development through PowerPoint presentations or other appropriate formats. Presentations do not need to be formal or elaborate and are only required when there is meaningful progress to share.
* Meetings with subject-matter experts and stakeholders may take place once or twice during the semester. These meetings may involve presenting the research and explaining the proposed AI-powered solution to a non-technical audience.
