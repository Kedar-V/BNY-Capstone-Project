# SEC merger filer sample

The CSV contains 28 distinct reporting filers drawn from SEC filings dated in 2025. It includes 23 Form 8-K/8-K/A filers and five Form 6-K filers. The sample spans all four calendar quarters and was selected from documents already downloaded during the broader all-filer `merger` search. Downloading stopped at 8,638 of the 9,900 selected candidate documents at the user's request. Seven initial sample rows were removed after review because they contained hypothetical, internal or historical transaction references. No replacements were added.

Each row identifies a reporting filer, filing date, form, CIK, accession number, direct SEC source URL and a short source excerpt containing merger-agreement or completed-merger wording. One filer appears at most once. The `merger_reference` field describes the quoted language; it does not establish current deal status. Some 2025 filings refer to agreements signed earlier in 2025. Reporting filers can be acquirers, targets or other transaction parties.

This is a **sample of filings**, not a count of distinct merger deals and not an exhaustive list of all mergers. It excludes simple mentions without the screened wording and does not identify buyer, target or consideration. Check the linked SEC document before using a row as a transaction record.
